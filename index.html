How to use

  1. Click Start camera and allow webcam access when prompted.
  2. Hold your hand clearly in front of the camera with good lighting.
  3. Try any gesture below — the matching card lights up in real time with a confidence score.

Tips for best accuracy

  • Lighting: bright, even light. Avoid strong backlight.
  • Background: a plain background behind your hand helps.
  • Distance: keep your hand 30–70 cm from the camera.
  • Angle: face your palm toward the camera for best results.

Privacy

All processing happens locally in your browser using MediaPipe Hands (WebAssembly + WebGL). Your video is never uploaded anywhere. The tool works offline after the model downloads once.
